Who We Help

Internationally Trained Dentists

Dentists educated outside Canada who are navigating Canadian licensing and career transition.

NDAEB Candidates

Candidates preparing for the NDAEB written examination, CPE, remediation, or additional-attempt requirements.

NDEB Candidates

Preparing for the AFK, ACJ, NDECC Situational Judgement, and NDECC Skills through the equivalency process.

Denturism Candidates

Individuals pursuing licensure in denturism in Canada, preparing for written and OSCE components.

Dental Professionals

Hygienists, assistants, and other professionals transitioning into the Canadian dental system.

Meet Ollie

I'm Oluchi "Ollie" Omogbai — a foreign-trained dentist, dental educator, and founder of License Ready with Ollie. I support internationally trained dental professionals as they navigate the Canadian licensing journey through education, examination preparation, mentorship, and practical guidance.

With more than 10 years of clinical and teaching experience in a university teaching hospital, I have taught and mentored junior dentists and final-year dental students. After moving to Canada, I continued my professional journey within the Canadian dental system while progressing through the licensing pathway myself.

My goal is simple: to make the journey clearer, more strategic, and less isolating — so you can understand your options, prepare with purpose, and keep moving forward.
