About the portal

Built for dental assisting students, not test-prep in general.

NDAEB Prep is a computer-based testing portal made for students preparing for the National Dental Assistant Examining Board assessment. Enroll, get your account activated, and work through a growing bank of board-style questions covering clinical dental assisting, safety, and professional ethics.

Every attempt is scored instantly and saved to your dashboard, so you can see exactly which sittings went well and which topics need another pass before the real exam.

What you get

Everything between enrolling and sitting the real board exam.

Timed practice exams

Sit a full multiple-choice mock exam, one question at a time, in the same format you'll face on the actual NDAEB assessment.

Instant scoring & explanations

See your score the moment you submit, plus a key-note explanation for each question so mistakes turn into things you now know.

Progress you can track

Every attempt lands on your dashboard with a date, score, and pass status, so you always know if you're ready.
